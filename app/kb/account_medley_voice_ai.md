# Account: Medley Voice AI (acct_medley)

## Overview
AI voice agents for collections and support, over audio streaming and Zentrunk, with Krisp noise
cancellation on. About 2 calls per second, spend about ₹35,000 per day. Media region ap-south-1
(Mumbai). Delhi-NCR-heavy customer base (many Vi-Delhi destinations, mostly routed via TATA by
least-cost routing). International calls use a +1 caller ID. HA enabled.

## Watch-outs
Because the agents stream media, moving agent or media infrastructure out of India breaks domestic
anchoring (4590) for India-to-India calls. Silent calls matter more than failures for an AI agent:
the bot talks to dead air.
